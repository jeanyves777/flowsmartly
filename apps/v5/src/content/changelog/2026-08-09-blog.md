more: /resources/blog

Posts are written in markdown and compiled to typed content, so every article
inherits the site's type scale and all three themes rather than arriving as
markup with styling of its own.

Each post exports as its own page, and the RSS feed, the sitemap dates and the
summary answer engines read are all generated from the same index.
