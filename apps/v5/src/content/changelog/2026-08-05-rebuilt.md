title: The public site, rebuilt on one design system
more: /product
---

One token set, one type scale and one breakpoint system replace 400 colour
literals, eleven body-text sizes and seven competing schemes that made
neighbouring sections disagree about when to stack.

Light, grey and dark are built from the theme rather than patched onto a light
design, and the site is positioned around what it is: the AI business operating
system.
