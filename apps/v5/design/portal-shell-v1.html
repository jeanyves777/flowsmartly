What changed from v1, and what stayed

Taken from your dashboard: the five-metric row with sparklines and a comparison period, quick create, the channel table, what is coming up, the pipeline, the storefront snapshot, the command bar beside search, and a rail that shows every product rather than hiding studios behind a hub.

Kept from v1: the credit meter in the rail and a credit cost on every recommendation, because the product is metered and a surprise bill is the fastest way to lose a small business. “Ran without you” stays too — it is the honest half of “human-approved by default”, and it is what makes the approval queue short enough to actually clear.

The chat now floats. Docked at the bottom of the canvas it shortened every page and scrolled away with the content. Floating, it is always on screen, collapses to a single bar that still reports what Flow.AI is doing, and the work it is waiting on you for sits inside the conversation rather than in a separate panel.

Where the five decisions stand

Two are settled by your dashboard. Three still need a call.

Does the rail show everything, or hide the studios behind a hub?

Your version lists every product, so that is what is built above — grouped, because twenty flat items is a long scan, but nothing is hidden.

Everything visible, grouped Settled

Six short groups instead of one list of twenty. Collapses to icons.

Where does the assistant live?

Floating chat, bottom right, always visible and collapsible — collapsed it still reports what Flow.AI is doing. The “Flow.AI is working” card stays on Home as the at-a-glance version.

Floating, collapsible Settled

Never shortens the page, never scrolls away. Command bar ⌘J opens it too.

How much is Flow.AI allowed to do without asking?

This is the one that matters. The public site promises “human-approved by default”, and “Ran without you” already lists four things that went out on their own. Those two need a boundary drawn between them, and it decides how the whole product feels.

Rules once, then it runs Recommended

You approve a kind of action once — “always answer the phone”, “never spend over $50/day” — and anything outside it waits.

Every action, every time

Safest, and unusable by week two: nobody clears forty approvals a day.

Do credits stay on screen?

Your dashboard does not show a balance anywhere; v1 put a meter in the rail and a cost on every recommendation. Both are on above, so this is a live choice — it is honest, and it also puts a price tag in the corner of your eye all day.

Meter plus per-action cost Built above

No surprise bills, and the cost of a choice sits next to the choice.

Only at the point of spend

Quieter. Move the meter to Billing and show cost only on the confirm step.

On a phone, is it the same product or a smaller one?

The phone shows four tabs, the queue, the numbers and the same floating chat — no studios. A video editor at 390pt is a large build for something few people will do standing up.

Approve and ask, not make Recommended

Phone handles the queue, the numbers and Flow.AI. Studios stay desktop.

Everything, everywhere

Full parity. Roughly doubles the build, and most studios will be cramped.
