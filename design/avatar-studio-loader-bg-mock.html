Why this fixes “dormant”

HeyGen's Avatar IV takes a natural-language motion prompt (custom_motion_prompt) that drives gestures & expression — the biggest lever on a lifeless read. Emotion + pace shape the voice. Better scripts (hook-first) carry the rest.

  • Emotion is voice-specific — pills grey out on voices that can't emote (no failed renders).
