General Computing Solutions (GCS) provides technology assessment and consulting services. Delivery of every engagement is handled as described below.

1 Delivery timeline

  • Standard Assessments: delivered within 5–7 business days of service completion.
  • Custom Architecture Review: delivered within 1 week (7 business days).
  • Expedited Delivery: available upon request for an additional fee.

2 Report format

All deliverables are provided as comprehensive PDF documents and may include supplementary materials — spreadsheets, visualizations, and supporting data — as applicable to the engagement.

3 Communication

Our team maintains regular communication throughout the engagement period. You'll receive status updates and have direct access to contact us with questions at any point.

Prefer a specific cadence or channel? Let us know at kickoff and we'll tailor updates to your team.

4 Expedited delivery

Need results sooner? Expedited turnaround is available for most assessments for an additional fee. Reach out before you order and we'll confirm timing and pricing.
