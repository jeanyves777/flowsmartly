Why this

  • Stay in flow. No need to navigate away — the build happens where you're already looking.
  • Live, not a spinner. Steps tick off and scenes appear as they render.
  • Minimal but real interaction. Preview a scene, tweak in one line, or open the full studio when you want more control.
  • Notifies + resumes. Leave and come back — the card reflects the latest state.
