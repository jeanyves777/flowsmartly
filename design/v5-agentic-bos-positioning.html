From assisted development to autonomous engineering

Conflicts with what the site claims today

Hero contradicts the LEAD RULE
Live: badge "Your AI marketing team — in one chat", H1 "One agent runs your whole marketing." That leads with marketing — the exact thing the packet forbids leading with.
Site metadata leads with channels
src/app/layout.tsx title "AI-Powered Content Creation & Marketing Platform"; keywords are social/email/SMS/ads; the JSON-LD SoftwareApplication describes a marketing platform. SEO says "AI marketing tool" while the page would say "operating system".
All nine surfaces collapse into one group
Every existing surface maps to Business & Growth. Four of the five capability groups have zero public surface today — the packet promises a breadth the site cannot yet show.
"Agent" is already taken
/hire-agent, /agent/* and the marketplace use "agent" to mean a human freelancer. "FlowAgent" and "specialized agents" collide with that meaning in the same product.
Named products were deliberately buried
LEGACY_SURFACE_REDIRECTS folds /flowshop→Sell and /listsmartly-details→Outreach. The packet re-elevates FlowShop and ListSmartly as named experiences — that reverses a shipped IA decision.
Both packet CTAs are new routes
"Explore FlowSmartly" and "Join V5 Early Access" have no destination. The hero's email capture currently posts straight to /register?email=. Early access needs its own route and capture, or the CTA lies.
Credits vs. enterprise
The live site sells on "Pay for work, not seats" and per-action credit pricing. The packet is silent on commercial model but talks to enterprises about policy, authority and architecture. Those two stories need reconciling before the pricing page is touched.
Dark-only is not a bug
MarketingShell hard-codes .dark, so the public site ignores the user's theme. This mock defaults to dark to match; the light/grey switcher is mock chrome only.

Wording guard applied throughout

"Agentic" describes the system. "Autonomous" appears only where the packet uses it — Autonomous Engineering and autonomous engineering as a workload. Every claim of independent action in this mock is paired with authority, approval or governance; nothing implies unlimited independent authority.
