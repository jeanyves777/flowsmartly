Minimal edit

Tweak the subject + body inline on the step card (what we have today). Fast.

Full edit →

Open the Pitch Studio playground: redesign the branded proposal, swap images, restyle, attach the PDF.

Selection: every contact has a checkbox — untick to exclude, tick to include; leads with no reachable channel are auto-excluded (shown faded). The header checkbox selects/clears all; search filters the list.
Run mode: Group enrolls all selected on the same sequence · One at a time staggers them · Just the selected one runs a single contact so you can test/execute one pipeline.
Customise: per contact, Minimal is the in-step editor we already have; Full edit opens the Pitch Studio playground for that contact’s proposal.
